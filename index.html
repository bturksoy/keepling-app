Private by default

Everything you write stays on your device. Keepling needs no account and no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you choose to turn on backup yourself.

If you do turn on Google Drive backup, the file goes to a private folder that only Keepling can read — it doesn't appear among your own Drive files, and nobody else can open it.

Optional, never required

  • Backs up automatically to your private Google Drive folder
  • Unlocks unlimited photos and voice notes
  • Lets you export the growth documentary
